Product Description
The RI30 KV260 is a compact frameless inner-rotor torque motor designed for high torque density applications. At just 40 g, it delivers up to 230 mN·m peak torque and features a Ø17 mm through-bore. It is well suited for quadruped robots, collaborative robot joints, robotic wrists, and precision medical systems requiring a balance of torque, size, and dynamic performance.

Product Highlights
Lightweight Design with High Torque Density
Weighing only 40 g, the motor operates at 24 V and delivers up to 230 mN·m peak torque, providing high torque density in a compact form factor suitable for dynamic motion systems.
Large Through-Bore Architecture
Despite its compact Ø38 mm outer diameter, the motor integrates a Ø17 mm through-bore, enabling efficient routing of cables, signal lines, or pneumatic tubing while supporting compact mechanical integration.
Frameless Design for Maximum Integration Flexibility
The frameless architecture allows the motor’s electromagnetic core to be directly integrated into robotic structures, high-ratio gearboxes, or fully integrated servo modules, enabling highly efficient system-level packaging and reduced overall system size.
Low Cogging Torque for Smooth Operation
Optimized low cogging torque reduces torque ripple across both low-speed and high-speed operation, ensuring smooth and precise performance in high-accuracy servo control applications.
