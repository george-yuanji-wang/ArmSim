Product Description
CubeMars RO40 KV140 Lite Outrunner Frameless Torque Motor As a lightweight version of the RO series, the RO40 KV140 Lite removes the outer casing and Hall sensors while retaining the core motor, aiming to provide developers with the most flexible integration solution.
This series fully inherits the outstanding electromagnetic performance of the RO series and delivers excellent smoothness even at extremely low speeds. The minimalist frameless design greatly cuts down motor weight and reduces system inertia, enabling versatile integration with precision joints, gimbals and custom reducers. Its large-diameter hollow allows multiple cables to pass through coaxially, making it a professional solution for applications requiring high power density and compact structure design.
