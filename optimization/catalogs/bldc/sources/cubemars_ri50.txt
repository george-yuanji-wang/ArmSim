Product Description
The RI50 KV100 is a frameless inrunner torque motor from CubeMars’ RI series, engineered for applications where space is limited but rapid response and high dynamics are essential—such as robotic joints, exoskeletons, collaborative robot arms, and AGV hub drives. Featuring high torque density, ultra-low cogging torque, and an exceptionally small form factor, it delivers outstanding dynamic performance and exceptional mechanical integration flexibility.

Product Highlights
High Torque Density
Continuous torque of ~0.58 N·m and peak torque of ~1.67 N·m meet the output and responsiveness requirements of lightweight robotic joints and modular actuators
Low Cogging Design
Optimized electromagnetic structure minimizes cogging torque, enabling smoother motion and higher control precision
Frameless Architecture
Supplied as separate rotor and stator components, allowing users to customize mounting structures and flexibly integrate with gears, flanges, or bearings
Compact & Lightweight
Weighing only ~180.8 g with an outer diameter of Φ54 mm and length of 27 mm, ideal for highly constrained spatial layouts
